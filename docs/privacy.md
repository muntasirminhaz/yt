# Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

yt is a Safari extension that adds playback and interface controls to youtube.com, with a small companion app. It is made by an individual developer.

## What is collected

Nothing. The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. It never sends anything to the developer.

## What stays on your device

- Your settings are stored by Safari in the extension's local storage on each device. Export and Import move them as a file you control.
- The extension reads the YouTube page in Safari to apply your settings. That happens on the device only.
- Privacy mode, when you turn it on, copies your youtube.com cookies into the extension's local storage and signs YouTube out. Turning it off puts them back. The copy never leaves the device.

## Optional community services

Three features are off until you turn them on. Each asks Safari for permission to reach its server the first time, and you can withdraw that permission in Safari's extension settings.

| Feature | Service | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Skip sponsor segments | SponsorBlock, sponsor.ajay.app | the first 4 characters of a hash of the video ID; never the full ID, your account or your IP beyond what any web request carries |
| Clearer titles from DeArrow | DeArrow, sponsor.ajay.app | the same hash prefix |
| Show dislike counts | Return YouTube Dislike, returnyoutubedislikeapi.com | the video ID |

These services are run by third parties under their own privacy policies: https://sponsor.ajay.app/ and https://returnyoutubedislike.com/.

## Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes the extension and everything it stored. Reset in the extension's settings clears the settings only.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
