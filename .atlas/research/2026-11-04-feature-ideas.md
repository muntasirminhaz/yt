## Notes

- **High demand but outside A–C:**
  - One-click "Hide channel" on cards: demand n=8 (community:348); Control Panel has it (firefox:598, App Store community:303).
  - Original audio track: community:355, status:57; Control Panel has it (firefox:602).
  - SponsorBlock submit and vote: status:52; SponsorBlock for Safari has it (apple:73).
  - DeArrow in feeds: status:53; DeArrow for YouTube has it (apple:249).
  - iPhone mini player and time remaining: parity demand n=10 (community:346); status:66.
- **Enhancer for YouTube parity** (shortlist:5-7): what remains is the macOS pop-up player (status:54) and its 50+ dark variants (chrome:66). Red Crow has both (apple:1452, apple:1459).
- **Spike first:** hide AI-labelled videos. Demand is HN 185 pts (community:73-75), and Weedout sells on the Mac App Store (community:77). Whether feeds expose the label is unverified; if not, each video needs its own lookup.
- **Dropped:**
  - Watching your own video without counting a view: views are counted server-side; Privacy mode only signs you out (status:32).
  - Studio analytics overlays: needs OAuth, and `identity` is unsupported (platform:43).
  - Lyrics: needs a licensed API (new, no citation).
  - Crossfade, EQ, mono: Web Audio can't process MSE audio (limits:5).
  - Rating bars on every thumbnail: the RYD API allows 100 requests a minute (status:94).
  - Translation and AI summaries: paid keys (status:64-65).
  - New-upload alerts: no `notifications` API (platform:43).
