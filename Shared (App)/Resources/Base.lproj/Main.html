Commander adds playback and interface controls to youtube.com in Safari: speed, loop, quality, autoplay, a cleaner page and more. Everything runs on this device.

Turn on the Commander extension in Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions, then allow it on youtube.com when Safari asks. Settings live in the extension's entry in Safari's page menu.
