Commander adds controls to YouTube in Safari.

  1. Turn it onSettings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Commander
  2. Allow it on youtube.comOpen a video in Safari, tap this icon in the address bar, then allow Commander.
  3. Find the controlsThey sit under the player. The same icon menu opens Commander's settings.

Turn on the Commander extension in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.
