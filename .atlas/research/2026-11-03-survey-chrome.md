## Enhancer for YouTube

- Features:
  - Playback speed via mouse wheel or keyboard, default speed, range 0.07x to 16x, finer steps than YouTube
  - Default playback quality for videos, playlists and embedded videos
  - Volume via mouse wheel; Volume Booster plus bass boost up to 10x
  - Default volume, speed and quality also applied to embedded videos
  - Enhanced Loop Mode (part of a video or the whole video)
  - Autoplay control (disable, block background-tab autoplay, fully stop videos to save bandwidth)
  - Disable high frame rate formats (force 24/25/30 fps)
  - Force MP4/AVC (H.264) instead of WebM/VP9
  - Video filters (brightness, contrast, color, more) and flip video
  - Enhanced Dark Mode with 50+ variants
  - Custom themes (custom CSS, accent colors) and custom script
  - Cinema Mode (dims everything around the player; Esc or click outside exits)
  - Wide Player (beyond Theater Mode, or fill the window without fullscreen)
  - Mini Player (auto-launches on scroll so comments stay readable)
  - Resizable Pop-up Player (can be pinned on top)
  - Picture-in-Picture toggle
  - Hide end screens, info cards, recommendations, related videos, live chat, comments
  - Hide Shorts or watch Shorts like regular videos
  - Screenshot capture of video frames
  - Reverse playlist order
  - Customizable keyboard shortcuts for most features (assigned in the browser shortcut settings; no defaults listed)
  - Mouse shortcuts: Ctrl+click = default speed; Ctrl+Shift+click = 1x
  - Settings backup/export recommended on the how-to page
- Needs external service/API: no (permissions: storage; hosts: youtube.com, youtube-nocookie.com)
- Known limits or complaints: how-to page lists no "1080p Premium" selection, booster can mute some avc1+mp4a streams until reload, wheel conflicts with Logitech Smooth Scrolling, flip not available in fullscreen with hardware acceleration, pop-up player needs the youtube-nocookie permission; recent reviews (2026-10/11) say it needs an update for the refreshed YouTube UI and "Convert Shorts" stopped working

- Known limits or complaints: store rating is only 2.50 across 19,872 ratings despite 5M users; page says a Chromium bug can break it and to disable then re-enable it in chrome://extensions; estimates are approximate for new/unpopular videos; YouTube removed the dislike API on 2021-12-13 so counts are modeled, not exact; recent reviews on page 1 are 5-star

## Summary

1. Most common features: Hide Shorts (13 of 43 products), default playback speed and default quality/Auto HD (10 each), loop, disable autoplay, configurable shortcuts, hide related videos, themes/dark mode and ad blocking (9 each), then theater/wide player and auto-skip of ads (8 each).
2. Unique to one product: crowdsourced sponsor skipping (SponsorBlock), crowdsourced titles/thumbnails (DeArrow), subscription groups/folders (PocketTube), virtual content country (Improve YouTube!), resume-where-you-left-off plus tracking-parameter stripping (YouTube Enhancer), Wi-Fi/cellular-aware quality (new Magic Actions listing), night mode for all sites plus game-controller support (Turn Off the Lights), parental controls (UnTrap), video download (Video Downloader Professional).
3. Depend on external services: SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app), Return YouTube Dislike (returnyoutubedislikeapi.com, also optional in Improve YouTube!), DeArrow (dearrow.ajay.app, thumbnail server, SponsorBlock, USD 1 license key), AI summaries (Glasp to OpenAI/Anthropic/Google/Mistral; UnTrap summaries), PocketTube sync (Google Drive/Google sign-in), RYS and UnTrap Pro, AdBlock and Adblock Plus Premium (vendor accounts), ad blockers (filter-list downloads), Ad Speedup and Stands (telemetry).
4. Clearly conflict with YouTube's terms: ad blocking (Adblock for Youtube, AdBlock, Adblock Plus, AdGuard, uBlock Origin Lite, Stands, Ad & Intro Skip, Improve YouTube!'s optional blocker, Iridium), ad speed-up/mute/seek-through (Ad Speedup, FadBlock, Ad Auto Skipper's paid mute) and video download (Video Downloader Professional itself disables YouTube because the Chrome Web Store forbids it); reviewers report YouTube's anti-adblock warnings.
5. Safari cannot do (native messaging aside): no downloads, sidePanel, notifications, identity, userScripts or bookmarks APIs (hits screenshot saving via downloads, UnTrap and downloader side panels, PocketTube/DF Tube notifications, PocketTube/RYD sign-in, uBO Lite and Global Speed userScripts); webRequest cannot block (DNR with rule limits only, no webRequest on iOS); Chrome-only tabCapture/offscreen (Global Speed boost and pitch); no contextMenus on iOS (BlockTube, Improve YouTube!); no OS-level always-on-top pop-out, global hotkeys or game-controller input.
